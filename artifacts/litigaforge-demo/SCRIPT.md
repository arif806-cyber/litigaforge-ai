# LitigaForge — Forge Workspace walkthrough

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** Show a new user how to move from a matter to a reviewed, saved Workspace without confusing analysis, research leads, or scenario scores for verified facts.
- **Identity:** LitigaForge primary navy `#1a2744`, teal `#0d9488`, amber `#f59e0b`; Space Grotesk display and JetBrains Mono interface labels.
- **Hero:** A fictional, anonymized Forge Workspace reconstruction; it is explanatory artwork, not a recording of a real user's case.
- **Motion:** Focused camera punches into the active tool; canvas edges trace to explain relationships; the amber cursor/focus ring is the recurring transition carrier.
- **Arc:** Orient → structure → analyze → verify research → pressure-test → save and audit. The finale resolves as a typographic route, not another feature screen.
- **Payoff:** A practical six-verb mental model with explicit boundaries: verify original sources; keep scenarios separate unless deliberately applying scores.
