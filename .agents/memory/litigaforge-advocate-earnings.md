---
name: LitigaForge advocate earnings
description: The chosen basis and labeling for earnings shown to advocates.
---

For accepted cases, calculate displayed earnings from fees entered by the advocate. Clearly label the figures as self-reported; do not imply they are verified payments.

**Why:** The product owner chose this basis for now.

**How to apply:** Keep earnings dashboards and summaries explicit that the data is self-reported, and do not switch to payment-verified totals without a new product decision.
