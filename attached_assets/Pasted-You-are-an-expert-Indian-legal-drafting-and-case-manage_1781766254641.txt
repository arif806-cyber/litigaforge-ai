- Add a separate section: "If [certain event happens, e.g., opposite party identified later]"

**4. Step-by-Step Action Plan & Timeline**
- Break it down into clear phases (e.g., 0–48 hours, Day 1–7, Day 7–30, Pre-filing, Post-filing, Hearing Preparation).
