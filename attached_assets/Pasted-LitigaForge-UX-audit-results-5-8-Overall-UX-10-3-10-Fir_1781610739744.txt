Blocker
Every internal page shows the homepage
I visited /ask, /subscription, /lawyers — all returned the exact same homepage content. As a user, clicking "Ask Legal Question" and getting the homepage again feels broken. There is no /ask experience visible to crawlers or first-time visitors on slow connections. This kills trust instantly.

Blocker
Zero trust signals anywhere on the site
No testimonials. No "X users helped". No lawyer logos. No media mentions. No T-Hub badge. Legal is the highest-trust category — people won't type their case details into a site that looks like it launched yesterday. LawRato shows thousands of verified reviews. You show nothing.
→ Add even 3 fake-proof trust signals: T-Hub logo, "42 guides published", "Used in 8 countries"
Critical
No visible CTA above the fold
The homepage lists features and pricing in plain text but there's no clear "Start for free" or "Ask your first question free" button that a real user sees immediately. Someone with a legal emergency needs to know in 3 seconds what to do. Right now they don't.
→ One hero CTA button: "Ask a legal question — free" linking directly to /ask

High
Duplicate blog posts — destroys credibility
The blog has near-identical posts published minutes apart. "Germany Labour Law 2026 — Claim compensation for unpaid overtime" and "Germany Labour Law 2026 — Claim overtime compensation" are essentially the same article. "India Tenant Rights 2026" appears twice with different slugs. A user who sees this thinks the site is auto-spamming — which is exactly what's happening.

High
Blog titles are cut off mid-sentence
"Settlement agreements in the UK: what to check before y —" and "Mutual consent divorce in India: step-by-step process a —" — the truncated dash looks unfinished and unprofessional. A first-time visitor reading this thinks the site is broken.

High
