Free Legal Advice & Aid (Government & Non-Profit)
If you can't afford a lawyer (income criteria apply, or for women, SC/ST, disabled, etc.):
NALSA.gov.in (National Legal Services Authority) → Free legal aid across India. Toll-free helpline: 15100. Apply via District Legal Services Authority (DLSA).b91d67
Telangana State Legal Services Authority (TSLSA) → For your area (Kukatpally/Hyderabad): Visit tslsa.telangana.gov.in or contact Hyderabad DLSA. They provide free lawyers in eligible cases.7294b9
Some platforms (LawRato, Legal Advice India) also offer limited free advice sections.
Best for Legal Research & Information (Judgments, Laws, News)

Quick Tips for Using These Sites
Filter by location → Most allow search for Hyderabad/Telangana lawyers.
Verify the lawyer → Check Bar Council of India enrollment (free search on barcouncilofindia.org or state bar sites).
Start small → Use free query/post feature first, then paid consultation if needed.
Reviews matter → Read Google/Trustpilot ratings and client testimonials on the platforms.
Serious cases → Online is great for advice, but court matters often need in-person lawyers eventually.
Apps → Most have mobile apps (e.g., LawRato, India Legal App) for easier access.
